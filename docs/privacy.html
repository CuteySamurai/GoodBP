Good BP Privacy Policy

App: Good BP (Android, package com.cuteysamurai.goodbp)
Effective date: 21 November 2026

The short version

  • Everything you enter stays on your phone.
  • The app has no internet access, no account, no ads and no analytics.
  • We (the developer) never receive your readings, medicines, photos or voice.
  • You can delete your data at any time.

What the app stores, and where

Good BP keeps the following only in the app's private storage on your phone:

  • Blood pressure readings: top number, bottom number, pulse, time, and whether it was entered by photo or by voice.
  • Medicines: name, a short description, reminder times, an optional photo of the pill, and a record of each reminder (confirmed by voice, photo taken, or missed).
  • Settings: name, age, and optionally weight and height, the doctor's blood pressure range, reminder times and language.

The app does not ask for an email, phone number, location, contacts, or any account.

Voice

When you speak a reading, the app uses your phone's built-in speech recognition service (for example, Google's speech service on most Android phones). Good BP receives only the text of what you said, uses it to fill in the numbers, and does not record or keep audio. Your phone's speech service may process your voice under its own privacy policy, which you can manage in your phone's settings.

Camera and photos

When you take a photo of your blood pressure monitor, the app opens your phone's camera app. The numbers are read from the photo on your phone. The monitor photo is kept only temporarily and is replaced by the next one. Pill photos you choose to take are kept on your phone so the app can show you which medicine to take, and are deleted when you delete that medicine.

Sharing

We do not sell or share your data with anyone. The app only sends data out of the app when you choose to: for example, when you tap to share a doctor's report (PDF) or a spreadsheet (CSV) and pick an app such as email or a messaging app. What happens after that depends on the app and the person you share it with.

Phone backup

If backup is turned on in your phone's settings, Android may include Good BP's data in your phone's own backup to your Google account. This backup belongs to you; we cannot see it. You can turn it off in your phone's settings.

Reminders and permissions

The app asks for permission to use the microphone (to hear readings), to show notifications and full-screen reminders, to set exact alarms, and to restart reminders after the phone restarts. These are used only for the app's own features.

Deleting your data

  • Delete a single reading: press and hold it in the history list.
  • Delete a medicine and its pill photo: open the medicine and tap Delete.
  • Delete everything: uninstall the app, or go to your phone's Settings, Apps, Good BP, Storage, Clear data.

Health information

Good BP is a logging and reminder tool. It is not a medical device and does not give a diagnosis or medical advice. Always follow your doctor's advice.

Children

Good BP is meant for adults and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If a future version adds a feature that sends data off your phone (for example, alerts to family members), we will update this policy before that version is released and change the date above.
